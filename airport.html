Airport Vocabulary — A2 Speaking Review

Teacher-led screen-share activity · 12 target words

1. Meaning check

Ask the student to explain or translate the word first. Then choose the best meaning.

2. Speaking: Airport journey

Imagine you are at the airport. Tell me what you do from the moment you enter the airport until you get your suitcase.

First…Then…After that…Finally…

Teacher: ask the student to use at least 6 target words. Do not show the word list immediately.

3. Situational questions

Ask follow-up questions. The student should answer in complete sentences.

4. Quick final check

Complete the sentence with a target word.

Teacher notes

  • Understand: student can explain the word and identify the place/process.
  • Recognise: student chooses the correct meaning but cannot explain it.
  • Needs review: student confuses the word or cannot identify it.

Important distinction: arrivals/departures are areas or information about incoming/outgoing flights; gates are specific boarding points; bag drop is specifically for leaving checked baggage.
